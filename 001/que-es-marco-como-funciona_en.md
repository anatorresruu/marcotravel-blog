# Goodbye to chaos: how MARCO was born, a new way to plan trips

Taking a trip should be the easy part. Choosing a destination, imagining everything you are going to discover, and starting to count down the days. However, for far too long, we have accepted that planning a getaway involves a little bit of stress.

Between the initial idea and the moment you pack your suitcase, an invisible wall usually appears, made up of open tabs, saved blogs, videos to watch, screenshots, incomplete maps, and endless group conversations.

One person recommends a restaurant, another sends a video about a hidden beach, and someone else saves a museum on the map. When it is time to organize everything, the information is scattered across different apps and conversations.

MARCO was created to change the way we prepare for a trip.

We are an artificial intelligence travel planner that helps turn an idea, a destination, and a set of preferences into a personalized itinerary. A tool designed to simplify the process and make planning a trip part of the experience itself.

## Planning a trip is still complicated

Today, we have access to more information about destinations than ever before: blogs, videos, social media, maps, and travel guides.

The problem is no longer finding information, but deciding what to do with all of it.

Looking for inspiration can be entertaining, but it quickly becomes an overwhelming amount of information that is difficult to manage. Recommendations can be repetitive, outdated, or poorly suited to the way we want to travel.

Not everyone wants to experience the same kind of trip. Some people prefer exploring a city without stopping, while others need a slower pace. Some travel in search of local food, others want to visit the main landmarks, and some prefer to move away from the usual tourist routes.

Our needs also change depending on whether we travel alone, as a couple, with friends, or as a family. Planning should not simply mean collecting interesting places, but turning them into a plan that makes sense for us.

## What is MARCO?

It is a travel planning platform that uses artificial intelligence to create personalized itineraries.

Instead of forcing you to jump between search engines, blogs, maps, and documents, MARCO brings the planning process together in one place. The aim is to help you move from a general idea to an organized trip in a simple, visual way that is adapted to your preferences.

We do not want to replace the excitement of discovering a destination or decide how you should travel. We want to simplify the work that exists between inspiration and the real experience.

MARCO helps you organize the possibilities and build a foundation for your trip. You remain in control and can adapt the result to your needs.

## How does it work?

Everything starts with a travel idea.

It could be a destination you have already chosen, a getaway you are just beginning to prepare, or a place you have always wanted to visit. From there, we use the information you provide to generate a tailored proposal.

The process is simple: you enter the destination, the dates, and some basic preferences. With that information, an organized itinerary is created for you to view in a clear and visual format.

## Designed for every kind of traveler

MARCO is designed for people who want to organize their trips independently but do not want to spend hours searching for, comparing, and organizing scattered information.

It can be useful for solo travelers, couples who need to agree on a plan, groups of friends who want to share an itinerary, or families with different interests, preferences, and travel rhythms.

It is simple and intuitive to use, so you do not need any experience with digital tools. We want both younger and older people to be able to create and view their trips comfortably, without complicated processes or previous knowledge of the destination.

MARCO can also help if you enjoy preparing every detail and need a better way to visualize the result, whether you already have a specific idea or are still exploring different possibilities.

Technology should simplify the process without removing its most personal side. That is why we would love MARCO to grow alongside a community of travelers who share experiences, recommendations, and opinions, helping us improve the platform and discover new ways to explore the world.

## The team and the idea

The idea came from a situation that many travelers have experienced: wanting to organize a getaway and ending up spending more time sorting through information than imagining the trip itself.

Behind the project is a team with experience in technology, design, data, business, and marketing, brought together by the same goal: to integrate every aspect of trip management into a single tool.

Our idea is constantly evolving. We continue to listen to users, improve the experience, and gradually introduce new features that make it even easier, more complete, and more personalized to plan, organize, and enjoy each trip. We want MARCO to evolve alongside the real needs of the people who use it.

We will continue to use this space to show you how the platform is evolving, introduce upcoming features, and talk about destinations, trends, and everything that makes traveling worthwhile.
