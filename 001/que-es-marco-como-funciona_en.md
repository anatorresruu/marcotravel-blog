# Goodbye to chaos: how MARCO was born, a new way to plan trips

<img src="/001/assets/marco-travel-planificador-viajes-ia.webp" alt="Introducing MARCO, the artificial intelligence travel planner." style="width: 100%; height: auto;" />

Taking a trip should be the easy part. Choosing a destination, imagining everything you are going to discover, and starting to count down the days. However, for far too long, we have accepted that planning a getaway means struggling a little along the way.

Between the initial idea and the moment you pack your suitcase, an invisible wall usually appears, made up of open tabs, saved blogs, pending videos, screenshots, incomplete maps, and endless group conversations.

One person recommends a restaurant, another sends a video about a hidden beach, and someone else saves a museum on the map. When the time comes to organize everything, the information is scattered across different applications and conversations.

MARCO was created to change the way we prepare for a trip.

We are an artificial intelligence travel planner that helps turn an idea, a destination, and a set of preferences into a personalized itinerary. A tool designed to simplify the process and make trip planning part of the experience itself.

## Why is planning a trip still so complicated?

Today, we have access to more information about destinations than ever before: blogs, videos, social media, maps, and travel guides.

The problem is no longer finding information, but deciding what to do with all of it.

Looking for inspiration can be entertaining, but it quickly becomes an accumulation of information that is difficult to manage. Recommendations can be repetitive, outdated, or poorly suited to the way we want to travel.

Not everyone wants to experience the same kind of trip. Some people prefer exploring a city without stopping, while others need a slower pace. Some travel in search of local food, others want to visit the main landmarks, and some prefer to move away from the usual tourist routes.

Our needs also change depending on whether we travel alone, as a couple, with friends, or as a family. Planning should not simply mean collecting interesting places. It should mean turning them into a plan that makes sense for us.

## What is MARCO?

MARCO is a travel planning platform that uses artificial intelligence to create personalized itineraries.

Instead of forcing you to jump between search engines, blogs, maps, and documents, MARCO brings the planning process together in one place. The aim is to help you move from a general idea to an organized trip in a simple, visual way that is adapted to your preferences.

We do not want to replace the excitement of discovering a destination or decide how you should travel. We want to simplify the work that exists between inspiration and the real experience.

MARCO helps you organize the possibilities and build a foundation for your trip. You remain in control and can adapt the result to your needs.

## How does MARCO work?

Everything starts with a travel idea.

It could be a destination you have already chosen, a getaway you are just beginning to prepare, or a place you have always wanted to visit. From there, MARCO uses the information you provide to generate a tailored proposal.

The process is simple: you enter the destination, the dates, and some basic preferences. With that information, MARCO creates an organized itinerary that you can view in a clear and visual format.

<img src="/001/assets/marco-travel-screenshots-mobile.webp" alt="Screenshots of the MARCO mobile app." style="width: 100%; height: auto;" />

The intention is not to provide a generic list of places. The aim is to build a proposal that takes into account how you want to experience the destination and the amount of time you have available.

Once the itinerary has been generated, you can review it and use it as a starting point. Through the website or the application, MARCO aims to support you both while preparing the trip and once you have reached your destination.

## From the traditional method to the MARCO method

The traditional method will probably sound familiar: dozens of open tabs, blogs lost in your browsing history, screenshots you never find again, videos saved across different social networks, a spreadsheet that is difficult to maintain, and endless discussions about what to visit first.

Each tool can be useful on its own, but none of them provides a complete view of the trip. Inspiration appears in one place, practical information in another, and organization ends up depending on documents that have to be updated manually.

The MARCO method starts from a different idea: bringing the planning process together in one place.

A platform that helps you move from an idea to an itinerary, organizes the trip visually, and allows you to check the plan whenever you need it, without removing your ability to decide or turning planning into a rigid process.

It is not about giving up research or ignoring recommendations. It is about having an organized foundation on which you can build your own experience.

## Who is MARCO designed for?

MARCO is designed for people who want to organize their trips independently but do not want to spend hours sorting through scattered information.

It can be useful for solo travelers, couples who need to agree on a plan, groups of friends who want to share an itinerary, or families with different preferences and travel rhythms.

It is also designed for people who enjoy preparing their trips and need a better way to visualize the result. You do not need to be a planning expert or know the destination beforehand. MARCO can help whether you already have a specific idea or are still exploring possibilities.

Technology should simplify the process without removing its most personal side. That is why we want MARCO to be a guide, not a closed set of instructions.

MARCO was born from a situation that many travelers have experienced: wanting to organize a getaway and ending up spending more time sorting through information than imagining the trip itself.

MARCO wants to help you reach that point with less effort.

No dozens of open tabs. No plans lost across different applications. No need to start from scratch every time a decision changes.

Just you, the destination, and a simpler way to organize everything you want to experience.
