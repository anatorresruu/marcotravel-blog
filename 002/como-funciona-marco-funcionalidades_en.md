# Getting started with MARCO: a tour of its basic features

In our first article, we explained why MARCO was created and how we want to simplify one of the most complicated parts of any trip: turning an initial idea into an organized plan that is easy to follow. Now it is time to take the next step and show you how the platform works from the inside.

When opening a new platform, it is normal to wonder where to begin, especially when you are still unsure about the destination, the dates or the type of trip you want to take. That is why we designed MARCO to guide you through the entire process, from the initial setup of your profile to the creation and personalization of an itinerary.

In this first tour, we will show you how to prepare your account, search for a destination through a guided process, discover recommended trips and access all the information from the platform’s different views. We will also explain how to adapt the initial proposal to your preferences and save your trips so you can return to them whenever you need.

## Set up your profile and adapt MARCO to the way you travel

Before you start creating itineraries, we recommend spending a few minutes setting up your profile. This space brings together the information linked to your account and allows you to manage the trips you prepare within the platform more easily.

Creating an account is free and allows you to save the itineraries you generate, so you do not have to start again every time you enter the platform. Once you have signed in, you will be able to access the **My trips** section, where you will find all the getaways you have added or created and return to them whenever you need.

Within your profile, you can also add your personal information and define aspects such as your usual travel budget, the pace at which you prefer to organize each day and the categories that interest you most, including food, culture, nature, leisure, shopping or wellness. You can also describe other, more specific interests or preferences to help MARCO tailor its recommendations more closely to you.

It is important to complete every step of your **Explorer profile**. The more information you provide, the more accurately the platform will be able to recommend activities and prepare an initial trip proposal.

These preferences will also be saved as a starting point for future searches. This means you will not have to specify what you enjoy, your preferred budget or your usual travel pace from scratch every time you plan a new getaway. You will still be able to review and modify those settings for a particular trip.

## How can you search for and configure a trip in MARCO?

Once your profile is ready, it is time to start planning. You can do this in two ways: by searching directly for a destination you already have in mind or by exploring the recommended trips available on the homepage for inspiration.

When you already know where you want to go, simply enter the destination in the search bar and begin the setup process. MARCO will guide you through a series of simple questions to understand the characteristics of the trip and prepare a proposal adapted to your needs.

The first step is to enter your travel dates or the number of days available. You can then specify who you are travelling with, whether you are going alone, as a couple, with friends or with family. This information is important because the pace, schedule and type of activities can vary considerably depending on your travel companions.

Next, you can review aspects such as your budget, the intensity of each day and the categories that interest you most, including food, culture, nature or leisure. You can also add more specific details to help the proposal match what you are looking for.

When you are still unsure where to go, the **recommended trips** on the homepage can help you discover new possibilities. These proposals allow you to explore different destinations, see how the activities are distributed and get a clearer idea of the type of experience each place can offer.

Using all the information collected, the platform generates an initial proposal organized by day. It is not a fixed itinerary, but a personalized foundation that you can then view, save and modify using MARCO’s different views.

## View and personalize your trip from its different sections

Once your trip has been generated, MARCO organizes all the information so that you can view and modify it easily. Instead of showing you an endless list of recommendations, the proposal is divided into different views, each designed to address a specific need before or during the trip.

### Itinerary

The **Itinerary** view is the main place for consulting your travel plan. Here, you will find the trip organized by day, with the different activities and plans distributed throughout each day.

This structure allows you to understand quickly what is planned without having to search through separate documents, maps or notes. You can move between the different days and consult the details of each activity while maintaining a clear overview of the entire trip.

The itinerary works as an initial personalized proposal. You can use it as it appears or modify it however you prefer by adding or removing activities and moving them between different days.

### Map

The **Map** view helps you understand where each of the activities included in the itinerary is located. This allows you to visualize how the different plans are distributed throughout the destination and check their locations more intuitively.

This perspective is especially useful while you are travelling, but it can also help during the planning stage. Seeing the different locations on the map makes it easier to understand the distances between them and get a clearer idea of how each day will unfold.

From this view, you can use the navigation feature to follow your daily routes without missing any of your planned activities.

### Build

The **Build** view is the space where you can personalize the initial proposal and transform it into a trip that suits the way you prefer to organize yourself. Although MARCO has already generated a selection of activities based on your preferences, the final distribution of each day remains in your hands.

When you enter this view, the different days of the trip appear with their available time slots, while the recommended activities remain saved so that you can place them wherever you prefer. This allows you to decide which activity to complete on each day, reorganize the order of visits and distribute the plans according to your schedule, priorities and travel pace.

You can also move an activity from one day to another, remove recommendations that do not suit you or leave free time whenever you prefer to enjoy the destination without following a fully structured plan.

The three views are connected: **Itinerary** allows you to review the plan, **Map** helps you locate it and **Build** gives you the control needed to modify it. This means you can switch perspectives depending on what you need at any given moment, without leaving the same environment.
