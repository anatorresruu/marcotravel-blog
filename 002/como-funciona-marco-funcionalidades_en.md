# Getting started with MARCO: a tour of its core features

In our first article, we explained why MARCO was created and how we want to simplify one of the most complicated parts of any trip: turning an initial idea into an organized plan that is easy to view. Now it is time to take the next step and show you how the platform works from the inside.

When you open a new platform, it is normal to wonder where to begin, especially when you are still unsure about the destination, dates or type of trip you want. That is why we designed MARCO to <strong style="color: #ff7f50ff;">guide you throughout the entire process</strong>, from the initial setup of your profile to the creation and personalization of an itinerary.

During this first tour, we will show you how to prepare your account, search for a destination through a guided process, discover recommended trips and view all the information through the platform’s different sections. We will also explain how to modify the initial proposal to suit your preferences and save your trips so that you can return to them whenever you need to.

## Set up your profile and adapt MARCO to the way you travel

Before you start creating itineraries, we recommend taking a few minutes to <strong style="color: #ff7f50ff;">set up your profile</strong>. This section brings together the information linked to your account and makes it easier to manage the trips you prepare within the platform.

Creating an account is free and allows you to save your generated itineraries, so you do not have to start again every time you access the platform. Once you have signed in, you will be able to access the <strong style="color: #ff7f50ff;">My Trips</strong> section, where you will find all the trips you have added or created and can view them again whenever you need to.

Within your profile, you can also add your personal details and define aspects such as your usual travel budget, the pace you prefer for each day and the categories that interest you most, including food, culture, nature, entertainment, shopping and wellness. You can also add more specific interests or preferences to help MARCO tailor its recommendations more closely to you.

It is important to complete every step of your <strong style="color: #ff7f50ff;">Explorer Profile</strong>. The more information you provide, the more accurately the platform will be able to recommend activities and create an initial trip proposal.

These preferences will also be saved as a starting point for future searches. This means you will not need to specify what you like, your budget or your preferred travel pace from scratch every time you organize a new trip. However, you will always be able to review and modify this information for a particular journey.

## How do you search for and configure a trip?

Once your profile has been set up, it is time to start planning. You can do this in two ways: by <strong style="color: #ff7f50ff;">searching directly for a destination</strong> you already have in mind or by <strong style="color: #ff7f50ff;">exploring the recommended trips</strong> available on the home page for inspiration.

When you already know where you want to travel, simply enter the destination in the search bar and begin the configuration process. MARCO will ask you a series of simple questions to understand the characteristics of your trip and prepare a proposal adapted to your needs.

The first step is to enter your travel dates or the number of days you have available. You can then specify who you will be travelling with, whether you are travelling alone, as a couple, with friends or as a family. This information is important because the pace, schedule and type of activities can vary greatly depending on your travel companions.

Next, you will be able to review aspects such as your budget, the intensity of each day and the categories that interest you most, including food, culture, nature and entertainment. You can also add more specific instructions so that the proposal matches what you are looking for even more closely.

When you are still unsure where to go, the recommended trips on the home page can help you discover new possibilities. These proposals allow you to explore different destinations, see how the activities are organized and get an idea of the type of experience each place can offer.

Using all the information collected, the platform generates an initial proposal organized by day. It is not a fixed itinerary, but a personalized starting point that you can then view, save and modify through the different sections.

## View and personalize your trip through its different sections

Once your trip has been generated, MARCO organizes all the information so that you can view and modify it easily. Instead of showing you an endless list of recommendations, the proposal is divided into different sections, each designed to meet a specific need before or during your trip.

### Itinerary

The Itinerary section is the main place to view your travel plans. Here, <strong style="color: #ff7f50ff;">you will find your trip organized by day</strong>, with the different plans and activities arranged within each part of your journey.

This structure allows you to quickly understand what is planned for each day without having to search through separate documents, maps or notes. You can move between the different days and view the details of each activity while always maintaining a clear overview of the entire trip.

The itinerary works as an initial personalized proposal. You can use it as it appears or modify it however you prefer by adding or removing activities and moving them between different days.

### Map

The Map section helps you understand where each of the activities included in your itinerary is located. This allows you to see how the different plans are distributed throughout the destination and <strong style="color: #ff7f50ff;">view their locations in a more intuitive way</strong>.

This perspective is particularly useful while you are travelling, but it can also help during the planning stage. Seeing the different points on the map allows you to understand the distances between them and get a clearer idea of how each day will unfold.

From this section, you can use the navigation feature to follow your daily routes without missing any of your planned activities.

### Build

The Build section is where you can personalize the initial proposal and turn it into a trip that suits the way you prefer to organize your time. Although MARCO will have already generated a selection of activities based on your preferences, the final structure of each day remains in your hands.

When you enter this section, the different days of your trip appear with their available time slots, while <strong style="color: #ff7f50ff;">the recommended activities remain saved so that you can place them wherever you prefer</strong>. This allows you to decide which activity to do each day, rearrange the order of your visits and distribute your plans according to your schedule, priorities and travel pace.

You can also move an activity from one day to another, remove recommendations that do not suit you or leave free time whenever you prefer to enjoy the destination without following a fixed schedule.

The three sections are connected: <strong style="color: #ff7f50ff;">Itinerary</strong> allows you to view your plans, <strong style="color: #ff7f50ff;">Map</strong> helps you locate them and <strong style="color: #ff7f50ff;">Build</strong> gives you the control you need to modify them. This means you can switch perspectives depending on what you need at any given moment without leaving the same environment.
